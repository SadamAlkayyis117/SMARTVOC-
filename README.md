# 🎮 SMARTVOC

### An Open-World 3D Educational RPG for Interactive English Vocabulary Learning

SMARTVOC is a **3D educational RPG game** that combines **open-world exploration, daily life simulation, interactive missions, and English vocabulary learning** into one immersive learning experience.

Unlike conventional learning methods that focus mainly on memorization, SMARTVOC encourages players to learn English vocabulary through **activities, exploration, interaction with NPCs, missions, reading, writing, and various gameplay challenges**.

> 🎓 **Learn English by playing, exploring, and experiencing the world of SMARTVOC.**

---

## 📖 About the Project

SMARTVOC was designed as an interactive educational game where players can explore an open world while gradually improving their English vocabulary skills.

The game integrates vocabulary learning into gameplay activities, allowing players to discover and learn new words through their experiences in the game world.

Players can interact with the environment, communicate with NPCs, complete missions, participate in mini-games, read learning materials, write reports, and collect vocabulary throughout their journey.

The main goal of SMARTVOC is to create a learning experience that feels more engaging and interactive than simply memorizing vocabulary from traditional learning materials.

---

# 🎯 Game Objectives

The main objective of SMARTVOC is to help players learn and improve their **English vocabulary** through an enjoyable and interactive gaming experience.

Players can:

* 📚 Learn English vocabulary through gameplay
* 🗺️ Explore an open-world environment
* 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Interact with NPCs
* 🎯 Complete missions and challenges
* 📝 Practice English through writing activities
* 📖 Access reading materials
* 🧩 Complete vocabulary-based mini-games
* 📘 Collect and review discovered vocabulary
* 🏆 Gain learning experiences through various activities

The more activities players complete, the more vocabulary and learning experiences they can gain.

# 📚 Vocabulary Learning

SMARTVOC focuses on learning English vocabulary through gameplay.

The vocabulary learning system is designed around **CEFR-based vocabulary standards**, allowing players to learn words progressively through interactive activities.

Instead of only reading or memorizing vocabulary, players can practice through several gameplay activities.

### Vocabulary Mini-Games

The game includes activities such as:

* 📝 Quiz
* 🔤 Word Scramble
* ⌨️ Speed Typing
* ✏️ Fill in the Blank
* 🗂️ Category Sort

These activities allow players to practice vocabulary in different ways and make the learning process more interactive.

---

# 📖 Interactive Dictionary

Every vocabulary item discovered by the player can be stored in an **Interactive Dictionary**.

Players can access the dictionary to review the vocabulary they have collected during gameplay.

This system allows players to revisit previously discovered words and continue learning at their own pace.

---

# 📝 Writing System

SMARTVOC includes a writing-based learning activity through a **Mission Report system**.

Players can complete certain missions and write answers in English as part of the learning experience.

This feature encourages players to not only recognize vocabulary but also actively use English in written form.

# 🎮 Gameplay

SMARTVOC allows players to experience English vocabulary learning through exploration and interaction.

A typical gameplay experience may involve:

```text
Explore the World
        ↓
Interact with NPCs
        ↓
Discover Activities and Missions
        ↓
Learn New Vocabulary
        ↓
Complete Vocabulary Challenges
        ↓
Collect Words in the Dictionary
        ↓
Continue Exploring and Learning
```

The game is designed so that learning becomes part of the player's overall gameplay journey.
